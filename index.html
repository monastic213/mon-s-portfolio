Projects

TechPath

In development

An intelligent platform for career path discovery and decision support among aspiring IT professionals at Cebu Eastern College, Incorporated. Team capstone project of five.

Many IT students are unsure which specialization fits them. TechPath assesses a student's skills, interests, strengths, and goals, then recommends suitable career paths. Students can also compare careers and see which skills they still need to build.

My contribution

Co-author of the study's first three chapters: the problem and its scope, the objectives, and the framework. I also work with my four teammates on planning the career assessment, recommendation, and comparison features.
